Klipra 0.16.1 Preview — Third-party components

FFmpeg and FFprobe are separate executables invoked by the Klipra media worker.
Mac builds: FFmpeg 9.0.2, Martin Riedl static GPLv3 builds.
Provider: https://ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de/
Build recipe: https://git.martin-riedl.de/ffmpeg/build-script
FFmpeg source archive: ffmpeg-9.0.2-source.tar.xz attached to this release.
External component versions and configuration: FFmpeg-macOS-build-details.txt in this archive.

Node.js is included as a separate JavaScript runtime for yt-dlp.
Source and notices: https://github.com/nodejs/node

Python, PyInstaller, yt-dlp, yt-dlp-ejs, NumPy, OpenCV, sherpa-onnx and their bundled dependencies retain their upstream licenses.
whisper.cpp and llama.cpp retain their MIT licenses.
Geist and Instrument Serif fonts retain their SIL Open Font Licenses.
YuNet face detector: license included in the archive.

Klipra application source and branding are separate from these third-party components.
